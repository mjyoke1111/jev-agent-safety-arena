Verified account · author-run notes · n=1Jiayuan “JY” Zhang: one day in, still skeptical

After a day of hands-on study, Zhang’s view was that Jev behaves like a much faster general classifier or decision-maker, a job LLMs already do, so the novelty faded quickly. With parameter scale undisclosed and probably small, he expects thinner world knowledge than a regular LLM and leaves complex-scenario accuracy as an open question.

His clearest sweet spot is a finite solution space plus low latency, where structured output can be checked programmatically. Computer Use fits the shape: DOM list → Jev action → new DOM list. But there are no benchmarks yet for reasoning quality or long-context computer use; he expects it to trail GPT-6 Astra on quality while winning on speed.

Rewriting parts of Pi Agent produced few wins. Parameterized tool calls, such as edits with exact line numbers, are not a finite set, so tool selection cannot simply be replaced. Compaction framed as classification looked possible but little different from using an LLM.

He sees more promise in hard real-time decision trees such as games, robotics and autonomous driving, where latency matters and training data can be scarce. He is building two tests: a Poker AI for adversarial model battles across long decision trees, and a Pokémon VGC doubles-battle AI trained from complete season data for use in ranked matches.

Not a benchmark Verified X account @jiayuan_jy, posted 18 Sep 2026; 55.2K views, 270 likes, 34 reposts. Faithful English summary of a Chinese-language post; no citable URL was supplied.
